Privacy Policy

App: Class Attendance (Asistencia a clases)
Last updated: September 2026

This privacy policy describes how the Class Attendance app handles user information.

1. Information we collect

The app does not collect or transmit any personal data to external servers. All data you enter (student names, classes, and attendance records) is stored only on your device.

2. How data is used

Data stored on the device is used exclusively for the app to function: recording and viewing attendance. It is never shared with third parties.

3. Advertising

The free version of the app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Google may use device identifiers to show relevant ads. For more information, see Google's privacy policy.

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the app will ask for your consent before showing personalized ads, through a message compliant with the industry's Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF). You can review or change your choice at any time from the Options → Privacy & ads menu inside the app.

4. Subscriptions

The app offers monthly and yearly subscriptions to unlock the Premium version. Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each period unless canceled beforehand. Transactions are processed by Google Play. We do not store or have access to credit card data or other payment information.

5. Canceling subscriptions

You can cancel your subscription at any time from your Google Play account settings. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period.

6. Permissions

The app may request access to device storage only to export and import data backups, and to export the attendance history to Excel. No other files on the device are accessed.

7. Children

This app is intended for adult teachers. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

8. Languages

The app is available in Spanish and English. You can choose the language manually from the Options menu, or let it follow your device's language automatically.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this same page.

10. Refunds

Google Play offers a 48-hour refund window from the time of a subscription purchase. After that period, the transaction is not refundable. To request a refund within the allowed window, please do so directly through Google Play.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can reach us through the reviews section on Google Play.
